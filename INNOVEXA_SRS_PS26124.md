# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

This SRS specifies the software behavior expected from the AI-powered onboard and centralized urban intelligence platform proposed for SIH 2025 Problem Statement 26124. It is intended to guide design, implementation, verification and acceptance.

## 1.2 Product perspective

The software is a distributed system comprising onboard edge software on public transport buses and a centralized control-center platform. It consumes multi-camera video and GPS/GNSS, converts observations into AI events, synchronizes them over available networks, stores them geospatially and presents them through web GIS and analytics interfaces.

## 1.3 Definitions

| **Term**   | **Meaning**                                                                             |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Edge node  | Onboard compute device connected to bus cameras and telemetry.                          |
| Event      | A structured, timestamped, geotagged observation generated by analytics.                |
| ANPR       | Automatic Number Plate Recognition / registration text extraction.                      |
| GIS        | Geographic Information System for spatial storage/visualization.                        |
| Evidence   | Image/clip/crop associated with an event.                                               |
| Confidence | Model-derived probability/score used to support review; not a guarantee of correctness. |
| Heat map   | Spatial aggregation visualizing concentration/intensity of events or congestion.        |

# 1. Source-Derived Product Context

The project transforms public transport buses into mobile urban sensing units. Multi-camera video is processed onboard to detect road defects, traffic conditions, infrastructure deficiencies, vulnerable pedestrian situations, and incident vehicles. The fleet-wide observations are aggregated centrally into GIS maps, heat maps, alerts, reports, route-delay analytics, and planning insights.

## 1.1 Source-defined capabilities

- Analyse front, rear, side and cabin camera streams on buses.

- Detect potholes, damaged roads, missing dividers, missing zebra crossings, damaged/missing traffic signboards, waterlogging and other road hazards.

- Detect, classify and count vehicles to estimate density and identify bottlenecks.

- Identify vulnerable pedestrian situations, including school children crossing roads.

- Track offending vehicles during incidents and extract registration number with confidence score, timestamp and GPS location.

- Aggregate fleet observations in a central GIS view and generate congestion heat maps, road-condition maps, incident reports and infrastructure-deficiency insights.

- Analyse origin-destination traffic patterns and estimate route delays.

- Minimize bandwidth by performing intelligent video processing at the edge and transmitting actionable events/evidence rather than continuous raw video.

# 3. External Interface Requirements

## 3.1 Camera interface

- The edge software shall support configured camera streams and uniquely identify camera position (front/rear/side/cabin).

- The system shall detect and report unavailable/stalled streams.

- The system shall preserve capture timestamps or create synchronized timestamps when source streams lack usable time metadata.

## 3.2 GPS/GNSS interface

- The edge software shall read latitude/longitude and timestamp from the configured GPS/GNSS source.

- The event generator shall mark location quality/staleness when a current fix is unavailable.

## 3.3 Network interface

- The edge software shall communicate over available 4G/5G/Wi-Fi using encrypted transport.

- Loss of connectivity shall not stop local inference; unsent events shall be queued subject to storage policy.

## 3.4 Web user interface

- The central platform shall provide an authenticated browser-based GIS/dashboard UI.

- The UI shall support map, time, event type, severity/confidence and fleet/route filters where metadata exists.

- The UI shall provide event detail with source evidence and AI confidence for authorized reviewers.

# 5. Use Cases

## UC-01 Review road defect

Primary actor: Road/Civic Officer

Preconditions: Authenticated user; road-defect events exist.

1\. Open road-condition map.

2\. Set time/location filters.

3\. Select a defect cluster/event.

4\. Inspect confidence, location, timestamp and evidence.

5\. Mark disposition/export/forward according to role.

Postcondition: Review decision is stored and audit trail updated.

## UC-02 Review incident vehicle

Primary actor: Authorized Incident Reviewer

Preconditions: Incident event and evidence uploaded.

1\. Open alert.

2\. Review event timeline and tracked vehicle evidence.

3\. Inspect ANPR candidate(s) and confidence.

4\. Validate/annotate outcome.

5\. Generate incident report or forward through approved workflow.

Postcondition: Reviewed incident status and audit record persist.

## UC-03 Operate offline and resynchronize

Primary actor: Edge Node

Preconditions: Network unavailable; local storage operational.

1\. Continue camera inference.

2\. Store event metadata/evidence in local queue.

3\. Monitor queue capacity.

4\. When connectivity returns, retry prioritized upload.

5\. Central ingestion deduplicates by event ID.

Postcondition: Queued events are synchronized or retained for retry/error handling.

## UC-04 Analyze congestion

Primary actor: Traffic Controller/Analyst

Preconditions: Traffic observations available.

1\. Open congestion layer.

2\. Choose time window and corridor/area.

3\. View heat map and bottleneck indicators.

4\. Drill into segment/route details.

5\. Export/report if authorized.

Postcondition: Selected analytics remain reproducible from stored observations and filters.

# 7. Data Requirements

- Every event must have a unique identifier, event class, timestamps, source device/bus/camera and AI confidence; GPS/location may be marked unavailable/stale rather than fabricated.

- Incident ANPR records must preserve candidate text, confidence and source crop/evidence reference.

- Geospatial coordinates shall use a documented coordinate reference system; WGS84 latitude/longitude is recommended for interchange.

- Central persistence shall support efficient filters by time, geography, event type, device/bus/route and review status.

- Raw/derived media retention shall be separately configurable from event metadata retention.

- AI model/version metadata shall be immutable for historical events.

# 8. Acceptance Criteria by Capability

| **Capability**    | **Acceptance evidence**                                                                                                                          |
|-------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Road defect       | A labeled pilot route dataset produces mapped detections with source evidence and measurable precision/recall; thresholds agreed after baseline. |
| Traffic density   | Known sample videos yield stable vehicle tracks/counts; central map shows aggregated segment/time indicators.                                    |
| Offline operation | Disconnect network during a field test; events continue locally and synchronize after reconnect without unintended duplicates.                   |
| ANPR incident     | Sample incident produces vehicle track, plate candidate/confidence, timestamp/GPS and evidence accessible only to authorized roles.              |
| GIS dashboard     | User can filter, map, drill into and review events; large-area queries remain responsive under agreed pilot load.                                |
| Security          | Unauthorized requests are rejected; role boundaries and audit records are demonstrated in acceptance test.                                       |
| Device health     | Offline camera/device conditions appear centrally within configured heartbeat/staleness window.                                                  |

# 9. Constraints and Dependencies

- Source presentation identifies Python, JavaScript, OpenCV, YOLO, NVIDIA Jetson/edge AI hardware, GPS/GNSS, PostgreSQL/PostGIS, React/web GIS, 4G/5G/Wi-Fi and a centralized cloud/control-center platform as the technical approach.

- AI performance depends on camera placement, resolution, lighting, road/weather diversity and available training/validation data.

- “Missing” infrastructure detection may require contextual/reference-map knowledge; the exact definition must be agreed during requirements validation.

- Route delay and origin-destination analytics require additional route/trip/schedule or movement context; the source does not define those interfaces.

- Legal/privacy/evidence retention requirements are deployment-jurisdiction decisions and are not specified by the uploaded materials.
